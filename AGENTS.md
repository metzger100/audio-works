Do not advance an affected implementation with a credible unresolved potentially blocking claim risk into prototype selection, manufacture or publication of implementation/build guidance. Document and investigate an alternative or applicable resolution. Preserve failed experiments and continue unaffected work. Do not provide a legal clearance claim; refer material unresolved legal interpretation for appropriate professional review before release.

No patent-holder contact, licence acceptance, payment, purchase or external publication is authorized by this instruction. Reversible repository research and documentation remain authorized within the user's task. Never silently relax project constraints to make a candidate appear eligible.
