# Repository agent instructions

Respect the owner's project constraints and existing evidence before making changes. Read the relevant project description and decision log. Meridian work also follows `meridian/lab/AGENTS.md` and its scientific/reproducibility rules.

**Standard, documented and currently obtainable parts are a core repository requirement.** Read `COMPONENTS.md`. Engineer repeatable implementations using ordinary production parts and actual purchasable nominal values, tolerances, ratings and packages. Verify dated private-customer small-quantity sourcing and delivered costs to Germany; record single-source risks and validate substitutes. Do not rely on NOS, obsolete or undocumented parts, selected specimens or hidden individual characteristics. Preserve unsourced concepts as research, but resolve implementation dependencies before prototype selection or build recommendations. After realizing optimized values or changing parts, preserve the proposal and re-run the complete applicable qualification suite. Keep procurement readiness separate from simulation and patent evidence; never invent current availability or prices.

**Patent non-infringement is a mandatory repository requirement.** Read `PATENTS.md`. Screen concrete implementations by relevant claims, current official status and territory; retain dated candidate-linked evidence. Do not infer clearance from originality, open-source licensing, private DIY use, visual differences or successful simulations. Track unresolved risks separately from engineering merit.

Do not advance an affected implementation with a credible unresolved potentially blocking claim risk into prototype selection, manufacture or publication of implementation/build guidance. Document and investigate an alternative or applicable resolution. Preserve failed experiments and continue unaffected work. Do not provide a legal clearance claim; refer material unresolved legal interpretation for appropriate professional review before release.

No patent-holder contact, licence acceptance, payment, purchase or external publication is authorized by this instruction. Reversible repository research and documentation remain authorized within the user's task. Never silently relax project constraints to make a candidate appear eligible.
