# Benchmark and prior-art track

No known microphone circuit has been cloned to seed the search. Phase 3 must establish a strong, properly optimized conventional JFET voltage-input reference, plus charge/TIA and feedback references with applicable manufacturer models. Give each the same P48/capsule/cable environment, no-selection population and full suite. Do not handicap the conventional control.

SCHOEPS' manufacturer manual (S03) currently supplies interface evidence and a complete-microphone comparison context, not a bare-capsule model or copied schematic. Cross-domain sources S05–S09 supply mechanisms and prior art. For future reference netlists record original publication, interpretation, model licences and configuration; do not infer a hidden production schematic from marketing claims.

Planned baseline families: self-biased/degenerate JFET voltage buffer; JFET feedback gain stage; CMOS-input charge front end; electrometer voltage buffer; analog carrier capacitance bridge. Optimizations and full corner noise/distortion must precede the intermediate architecture comparison milestone.
