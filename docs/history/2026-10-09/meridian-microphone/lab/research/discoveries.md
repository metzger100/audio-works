Those results do not reject JFET, BJT/no-FET or charge sensing as classes.

The carrier backend must save the actual local-ground reference. Live stock, missing exact MOQ/film EUR price/delivered fees, passive loss laws, joint semiconductor populations, safe capsule polarization, physical capsule noise/polar/overload, independent preservation and candidate claim/official-status screening all remain unresolved. Primary sourcing observations and dated candidate patent feature flags are separate records; no build-ready or legal-clearance claim follows from these experiments.

- Successful simulator exit codes can coexist with rejected or absent analyses. The harness checks logs, complete raw data, required vectors, physical oracles and numerical settling.
- P48 power availability depends on current, feed/cable resistance and output behavior. The passive fixture draws about 2.75 mA and produces about 38.5 V internally from nominal 48 V; this matches the loaded-feed calculation.
- Merely using symmetric output resistors does not guarantee impedance balance. In the fixture, capsule/backplate supply coupling creates common-mode-to-differential conversion and unequal diagonal impedances. Disabling the ideal signal gain recovers the analytically symmetric output network.
- Capsule polarization-network noise can matter strongly. It must be separately integrated, not hidden within a convenient single weighted number.
- Capsule capacitance, safe polarization, sensitivity and mechanical noise remain unknown for the current Flat K47. A different regular K47's 70 pF product claim is not transferable.
- A handful of successful Monte Carlo samples cannot establish 99.9% yield. The software reports failures in the denominator and an exact binomial interval; assumed scenario distributions are not production statistics.
- The scaled charge observer with a native capacitor and Gear-2 realizes the imposed charge law while passing AC/transient agreement and timestep-refinement controls. The earlier native-Q and series-motion numerical failures do not establish that charge sensing is physically unsuitable.
- Harmonic estimates depend on resampling as well as the SPICE timestep. Linear interpolation introduced artificial harmonics; cubic-spline coherent fitting passed the unchanged refinement test. The ideal fixture's very low THD is a numerical control, not a microphone prediction.
- Constrained numerical optimization can improve feasibility while worsening other objectives. In the final same-suite fixture comparison, response deviation improves from 3.882 to 0.994 dB, while conditional electronics noise and impedance balance worsen. The full suite rejects the proposal and the eligible Pareto front stays empty.
- The 202-trial differential-evolution exercise reached its iteration budget without meeting its convergence criterion. It demonstrates executable numerical search, not global optimality or robust worst-case optimization.
- A source-manifest change during evaluation invalidates the run even when simulations finish. The affected experiment was retained and repeated with stable sources; proposal exports should happen before evaluators start.
- The exact ngspice DRAM-estimate advisory is retained in job records but does not itself imply a failed analysis. Convergence warnings, truncation, missing vectors, nonfinite data and unknown warnings remain failures; regression tests cover both warning classes.
- Final reporting review found that early percentile summaries filtered on requirement pass rather than completed simulation. Although the pass denominator retained failures, worst-case metrics were understated. The implementation now includes every finite completed pass/fail metric, reports missing/error counts and has an explicit adverse-specimen regression. Earlier records remain immutable and their percentile summaries are superseded by the corrected final runs.
