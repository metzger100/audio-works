# Hypotheses

The [catalogue](../search/concepts.yaml) is a queue of falsifiable physical proposals, not a measured population or qualified design. The [first batch](first_architecture_batch.md) now records six implemented mechanisms and preserved children.

H0: physics identities and deliberate bad-netlist cases can distinguish valid simulations from plausible-looking failures.

H1: optimizing a deliberately weak **ideal fixture** is useful for proving numerical optimization and full-suite rejection. It provides no semiconductor or architecture advantage claim.

H2 — conventional voltage input: fixed JFE150 gate divider/source degeneration can establish bias without selection.0001→0007 preserves conversion while replacing failed output macros;0007→0015 tests the measured dominant backplate-feed resistor noise with an ordinary100nF bypass. All remaining budgets and device populations still apply.

H3 — feedback JFET: a separate slow source-current servo can reduce specimen-sensitive DC bias while retaining voltage sensing.0002→0008 tests a real NPN differential controller; its base currents, divider error, rail dependence and startup are not idealized away.

H4 — guarded CMOS voltage input: external guarding/bootstrap can reduce parasitic/leakage loading, but cannot erase intrinsic input capacitance or guard noise.0003→0010 preserves that mechanism; actual OPA macro convergence remains unresolved.

H5 — CMOS charge feedback: a real capacitive return plus parallel resistor can provide displacement-charge gain and DC leakage authority.0004→0011→0014 tests output loading and a physical C0G leakage/loss realization, while preserving failed models and unscreened patents.

H6 — all-BJT input/audio/power: discrete charge feedback can operate without a FET, but base-current authority, finite gain, reset conductance and noise may defeat budgets.0005→0012 tests lower reset resistance using two parallel ordinary1MΩ parts. It is not representative of every no-FET topology.

H7 — floating bridge: carrier sidebands and synchronous recovery should obey charge conservation.0006→0009→0013 preserves integration failures and explicit ideals. An isolated runnable bridge/demodulator control separately tests the mechanism, nuisance-tone extraction and timestep convergence; full periodic noise/power and P48 integration remain unresolved.

No hypothesis retires a family or constitutes performance, sourcing, safety or legal clearance. See each candidate's nine questions and immutable result links in the batch report.
