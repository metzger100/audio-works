# Carrier bridge protocol — 2026-10-09

This is a deterministic mechanism protocol, **not validated periodic noise qualification**. See [batch report](first_architecture_batch.md), [backend](../src/meridian_lab/architectures.py), [independent controls](../tests/test_architectures.py), [bridge parent](../candidates/candidate_0006/notes.md), [ideal-buffer child](../candidates/candidate_0009/notes.md) and [passive realization](../candidates/candidate_0013/notes.md).

The ordinary DUT ports, P48 feeds, capsule charge-conservation model, cable and preamp remain attached. A floating 100kHz excitation of 0.2V peak is added to the backplate; the reference capacitors see anti-phase excitation. Finite capsule/reference imbalance is retained. Synchronous multiplication and the unresolved oscillator are explicitly ideal controls. Candidate0006 uses real OPA197 macro buffers/drivers; candidate0009 replaces those macros with explicitly ideal controls after convergence failure, preserving the parent. Candidate0013 additionally realizes the 100pF parts as Yageo C0G with explicit leakage/loss scenarios. None can be qualified for hardware power/noise/clipping.

Run operating point and terminal-energy accounting first. Then save a 20–30ms window after a 20ms electrical settling interval, with Gear2 and the existing solver tolerances, using maximum timesteps of 1/(32fc) and 1/(64fc). Do not reduce the frozen capsule uncertainty ranges. Four controls are retained: zero pressure/carrier on, pressure on/carrier off, pressure/carrier on at both timestep densities. Pressure is 1Pa peak at1kHz. Simultaneously fit DC, trend, fa,2fa and fc/2fc±fa/2fa. Direct audio and mixer images must be represented: restricting the basis produced a preserved biased extraction on adaptive sample times. The circuit's two low-pass sections retain their actual loss. No audio-band, acoustic polar, stability or stochastic-noise score is inferred from this one tone. An [isolated control](carrier_island_control.py) uses a separate single-RC analytic oracle; it does not qualify the integrated P48 DUT.

Independent tests use an analytic AM product-to-sum oracle and a separately excited passive capsule island with charge conservation. For C0=70pF and Cref=100pF, the small-motion sideband peak is Vcarrier*C0*Cref/(C0+Cref)^2*(Sref/Vref)/2. The isolated zero-parasitic control checks this relation with 2% analytic agreement and 0.5% timestep agreement; these are additional regression criteria, not amendments to microphone acceptance budgets. Its near-zero parasitics are explicit analytic-control assumptions; candidate application envelopes are unchanged.

Qualification remains incomplete until a physical oscillator, excitation, input buffers, multiplier and output stage have documented obtainable BOMs and validated device behavior. A future periodic-noise backend must establish periodic steady state, device noise spectra under periodic bias, RF sideband folding and correlations, carrier AM/PM and clock feedthrough, stochastic injection with documented seeds, anti-alias bandwidth, demodulator phase, filter equivalent-noise bandwidth, PSD confidence/convergence and timestep refinement. Validate against a calculable noisy bridge/thermal-resistor oracle, carrier-off stationary equivalence and an independent solver or measurement. Count supply and output-driver noise/current as well as signal-path contributions. Ordinary DC-linearized `.noise` cannot substitute for this protocol.

An unsupported carrier-noise test is a protocol gap, never a rejection of the floating bridge's physical conversion principle. Unexplained coherent output in a zero-pressure control must be investigated as feedthrough or simulator artifact. Complete patent screening, capsule safety evidence and private Germany sourcing remain independent gates.
