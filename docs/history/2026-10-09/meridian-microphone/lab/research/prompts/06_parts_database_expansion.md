# Prompt 6 — Expand the parts and model databases by circuit role

Work in `meridian-microphone/lab` on M100 Meridian MIC-34-M/MIC-34-C, K47FRB under P48. Read and apply [the continuation execution contract](RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md), including mandatory extensive web research, absent capsule measurements, no-selection, procurement/patent gates and completion checks. Use root reading route `device-models`. This is research/library expansion, not a shopping list or production selection.

Inspect the current semiconductor registry, acquisition/reference/corner records, `research/device_model_validation.md`, existing procurement registers, passive BOMs and `research/architecture_comparison_001.md` before adding entries. The drafting baseline has seven device entries, nine successfully acquired hash-pinned SPICE files over five device types, a retained failed HTML download and fourteen exact passive MPNs. Four JFE150 model variants represent one device; MMBFJ113 has no acquired model. Verify this baseline rather than assuming it is still current.

Build an expanded, searchable parts evidence database that improves the ability to test genuinely different mechanisms and realistic support circuitry. Record investigated, acquired, characterized and behavior-validated states independently from lifecycle/sourcing and candidate-combination qualification. An interesting part without a model may remain a research lead; it must not receive simulated performance or silently become an implementation dependency.

## Research and implementation

1. Create a coverage/gap matrix by **circuit role**: high-impedance voltage/charge input; low-noise bipolar amplification; high-voltage P48 power/current control; low-noise balanced output; CMOS/electrometer/guard/servo; carrier excitation/demodulation; protection; high-R/low-leakage passives and practical coupling/compensation capacitors. Identify gaps that currently prevent an experiment, and prioritize closing them over collecting names.
2. Perform broad manufacturer/model/lifecycle research across independent suppliers and device families. Investigate further discrete JFETs and integrated duals, starting with documented leads such as JFE2140 and J113/MMBFJ113 and other current manufacturer catalogues. Investigate ordinary bipolar alternatives, including **2N5551/MMBT5551/PMBT5551-class NPN parts and 2N5401-class PNP parts**, with exact manufacturer/package suffixes. These are BJTs, not JFETs. Keep low-noise input merit separate from high-voltage power/output merit. Manufacturer-guaranteed matching in an integrated part is assessed under ordinary documented production variation; no selected pairs or matching service is allowed. Historical names such as 2SK170, BF862 or 2N5457 are search leads, never assumed-current dependencies.
3. Extend MOS/CMOS and amplifier research where it can unblock charge, guard, floating or power/output controls. Seek primary model guides, noise spectra at relevant bias/current, capacitance conditions, bias/leakage behavior, supply current and common-mode/overload limitations. A model that omits flicker/leakage or fixes offset/current is not evidence those effects are zero. Do not transfer noise measured at another current, source impedance, frequency or supply without an explicit derived model and uncertainty.
4. Expand standard-passive coverage as needed by the actual hypotheses: preferred nominal values, tolerance/TCR/voltage coefficient/excess noise, high-R leakage/insulation, capacitor loss/leakage/absorption, polarity and package dimensions. Audit the existing 1GΩ, film/C0G and parallel-electrolytic dependencies. Distinguish datasheet limits at one frequency/voltage from a broadband/temperature law. Exact substitutes and combinations need their own implementation qualification.
5. For each promising exact part retrieve datasheet, original manufacturer SPICE/TINA/PSpice/archive/model guide and applicable documentation where publicly accessible. Verify file type, pin order, units, included dependencies and unencrypted runnable content; retain hashes/versions/notices and failed acquisitions. Extend the existing registry/acquisition workflow compatibly rather than creating an unrelated catalogue. Store unresolved-redistribution bytes privately with reproducible acquisition metadata.
6. Record exact private-customer Germany small-quantity routes, lifecycle, dated stock/orderability, MOQs/packs/lead times and intended-use costs. Seek independent routes or practical substitutes; disclose single-die/source dependencies. A model from another manufacturer, an alternate package, or an indexed stock count is not validated interchangeability or live orderability. Do not invent prices or recommend purchasing comparison stock.
7. Add a small finite set of isolated characterization controls for the most useful additions, selected by the stated role gap and primary evidence. Compare DC, capacitance, temperature, leakage, voltage/current/1/f noise and transient behavior only where independent references exist. Use bias grids and coupled corners relevant to Meridian, not favorable hand-selected conditions. Preserve numerical errors and scope limitations. Defer unsupported behavior rather than fitting a generic device to a desired microphone result.
8. Research component-related technical prior art and flag relevant features/model terms separately. Exact implementation claims are screened under Prompt 11; adding a public model does not provide patent clearance. Update the source-linked concept/knowledge handoff for mechanisms enabled or still blocked by each part.

Useful starting portals, to refresh at execution: [TI JFE150](https://www.ti.com/product/JFE150), [TI JFE2140](https://www.ti.com/product/JFE2140), [Nexperia PMBT5551](https://www.nexperia.com/product/PMBT5551), [onsemi](https://www.onsemi.com/) and [Vishay](https://www.vishay.com/). These are discovery routes, not completed exact-stock/model validation.
