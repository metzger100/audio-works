## Source research and controlled diagnosis

1. Research original manufacturer archives, extraction notes, model guides, errata, application/reference decks and simulator documentation. Follow [TI JFE150 model resources](https://www.ti.com/product/JFE150): generic SLPM366, PSpice SLPM349I, transient reference SLPM358, TINA model SLPM350B and reference SLPM351B are discovery identifiers to verify, not guaranteed identical current files. Compare exact revisions/archive members with acquired hashes. Determine whether models share core parameters or represent independent extractions; agreement between duplicate cores is not independent validation.
2. Use [ngspice documentation](https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/docs.html) for the installed version, PSpice compatibility, devices, switches, global-ground references, integration and convergence. Preserve the installed baseline and exact options. Other documented backends may supply separately labelled cross-checks when available and permitted; do not install/accept proprietary licences or pool their results as one cohort. Search concrete error mechanisms and trace primary references rather than indiscriminately changing tolerances.
3. Build terminal-level **isolated JFE150 fixtures** with known voltages, loads, source impedance and initialization, covering DC transfer/output, gm, capacitance/admittance, leakage, terminal current/energy, voltage/current/flicker noise and transient response. Match reference conditions exactly, separate typical curves from guaranteed limits, and compare generic/PSpice/TINA variants on identical conditions. Study clamp/switch state, pin mapping, current sign, shared definitions and native noise bookkeeping. Test nominal/weak/strong files as complete coupled hypotheses; preserve their existing guaranteed-limit disagreements. Do not fit arbitrary favorable corners or count these files as a measured population.
4. Build isolated **bipolar fixtures** for original BC846B, documented child and available independent manufacturer alternatives from Prompt 6. Investigate emitter connectivity, quasi-saturation network, pin order, gain grade, base-current authority, capacitance, bandwidth, temperature, supply/terminal current, noise and saturation/recovery. Preserve the original broken parent. Structural/parameter repairs require exact diffs, ancestry, a physical rationale and independent references. Missing KF/1f or joint process data remains unqualified. A successful one-bias beta fit is not general validation.
5. Progress from an independent resistor/RC/charge or simple active-device transient oracle to a minimal stage, then the original microphone DUT. Freeze each plan/source set before runs. Diagnose discontinuities, floating references, macro switching, unrealistic initialization, terminal loads and analysis/extraction bugs separately from physical instability. Restore zero-current observers or fixtures only through explicit children; no concealed bias assist, ideal replacement or hidden resistor may earn real-device performance.
6. Establish settled **nonlinear extraction controls**: calibrate amplitude/RMS/peak conventions and harmonics using known linear and synthetic-nonlinear signals; verify H2/H3/H4–H10, THD, window/phase/coherence and output fundamental. Require a justified settled interval, retained startup cycles, timestep/output-sampling/window refinement and uncertainty. Capture the entire original waveform and solver log. Unsettled/partial data receives no harmonic/headroom credit. Respect existing timeout/refinement/acceptance budgets; exhausted controls remain failures rather than a reason to raise limits silently.
7. After the isolated controls support the required behavior, run affected original candidate comparisons under a new common frozen cohort. Preserve old failed experiments and model identities. Keep electronics clipping/headroom bounds separate from uncertain capsule mechanics; right-censoring remains a bound. If a real-input model stays unsupported, supply the precise reduced reproducer, affected gates and an independently supported alternative research route.
8. Research noise-model extraction and high-impedance instrumentation knowledge, and carry model/licensing and feature/patent flags separately. No public download, cross-backend agreement or repair provides production qualification or legal clearance.
