# Broadening M100 beyond microphones

**Working proposal — 5 October 2026.** The recommended edits are applied to the [draft brand guide](../README.md) and the current manufacturer lockups. The previous guide and lockups are [archived for comparison](../archive/2026-10-04-microphones/README.md).

## Recommendation

Use **METZGER100 AUDIO** as the full brand name, **M100** as the principal mark, and **Studio & recording equipment** as an optional explanatory descriptor. Put the actual equipment category beneath or alongside each product name.

This keeps the personal identity and the existing vector artwork while covering microphones, analog preamps, interfaces, equalizers, monitor controllers and other audio equipment. The only wording that constrains the current logo is MICROPHONES; the mountain, beard, lettering, Art Deco structure and five-colour palette already work beyond that category.

## Naming options

| Full brand name | What it communicates | Tradeoff | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **METZGER100 AUDIO** | Broad audio design identity with a direct link to the existing name. | “Audio” is broad; descriptive copy should establish the focus on recording equipment. | **Preferred.** Short, flexible and straightforward. |
| **METZGER100 AUDIO WORKS** | An independent workshop making audio equipment. | More emphasis on craft; longer in small markings and introductions. | A strong alternative if the workshop character matters most. |
| **METZGER100 STUDIO EQUIPMENT** | Clearly describes the intended product range. | Long as a logo subline; studio positioning is less accommodating of future location or live equipment. | Best as explanatory language, rather than the full name. |

“M100 Audio” can be a compact spoken or written form, but retain the full **Metzger100 Audio** identity on first introductions, document titles and searchable metadata. M100 should be understood as the brand mark, rather than the model number of the first microphone.

A quick search on 5 October 2026 found existing audio products using M100, including the [RIG M100 microphone](https://www.riggaming.com/m100) and [Cambridge Audio's Melomania M100](https://www.cambridgeaudio.com/usa/en/faqs/what-difference-between-a100-and-m100). This supports retaining Metzger100 in the full name for recognition and search context. The search is not a determination of domain or trademark availability; neither has been assessed here.

The [comparison preview](preview.html) shows the existing MICROPHONES lockup, the recommended AUDIO lockup, and both alternatives with the same M100 lettering and divider. All alternative sublines are outlined SVG lettering.

## Proposed edits

| Existing direction | Proposed edit | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| METZGER100 MICROPHONES | **METZGER100 AUDIO** | Covers every planned equipment category. |
| M100 treated as a product brand beneath the manufacturer | M100 is the common visible identity; Metzger100 Audio is its full name | Avoids making M100 look like one microphone model. |
| “Reference sound with character” as the umbrella promise | **“Audio equipment with precision and purpose.”** | Encompasses acoustics, gain, conversion, controls and reliability. |
| Every product is slightly warm | Define sonic behavior per product | Leaves room for a transparent interface and a deliberately voiced preamp. |
| “Precision. Character. Forever.” | **“Precision. Character. Built to last.”** | Connects longevity to serviceability and design choices. “Forever” can remain an alternative if its poetic tone is preferred. |
| Four predefined microphone families and capsule-based variant codes | Categories + product names + documented variants | Lets future products develop their own names without importing microphone-specific specifications. |
| Manufacturer lockup across the full 480-unit subline width | Centre the shorter AUDIO subline across **360 units**, retaining its 20-unit cap height | Preserves restrained tracking rather than stretching a much shorter name across the old width. |
| Colour artwork used for every marking | Add **Graphite and Ivory single-colour wordmarks** | Supports one-ink front panels, PCB silkscreen and engraving. |

## Product architecture

The hierarchy stays the same on each device:

> **M100** — brand  
> **MERIDIAN** — product name  
> **CONDENSER MICROPHONE** — function

A future preamp or interface uses M100, its own product name, and a plain functional descriptor. There is no need for separate “M100 Preamps” or “M100 Interfaces” brands. Meridian remains the microphone's name; it does not have to stretch across unrelated devices.

Keep Vesper, Atlas and Regent in the naming reserve. Assign them when the actual product makes the association useful. Future categories and names are possibilities, not an announced roadmap. The Meridian engineering project and its decision gates are unchanged by this branding draft.

## Visual application

Keep the mountain, striped peak, geometric beard, custom M100 lettering, Art Deco divider and existing palette. Their identity remains distinctive on microphone bodies and electronics enclosures.

Use the full emblem on packaging and larger surfaces. Use the tall badge on suitable vertical areas. Prefer the horizontal wordmark on preamp and interface front panels, with readable product names, channel numbers and control labels beside it. A precise sans-serif face can serve functional labels while the outlined serif capitals remain in the brand subline.

The single-colour wordmark keeps the same contours and counters; only the paint changes. The existing full-colour background rule remains: standard artwork on Ivory and Graphite, inverse artwork on Forest Green. The filled monogram keeps its own colour construction.
