# METZGER100 MICROPHONES

## Brand concept and artwork guide

> **Precision. Character. Forever.**

This guide defines the brand idea, product architecture, visual identity and use of the approved M100 artwork. The editable SVG masters live in [design/](design/); the [preview sheet](design/preview.html) shows their application on the three brand backgrounds. Detailed geometry, reproduction dimensions and rebuild instructions are documented in [design/README.md](design/README.md).

## Brand Idea

**Metzger100 Microphones** combines technical precision, craftsmanship and a subtly warm, musical character.

The brand should feel like an independent European manufacturer of high-end studio equipment: premium, durable, technically credible and visually shaped by **Art Deco, classic studio equipment and modern precision**.

The positioning deliberately sits between two extremes:

- not clinically neutral
- not artificially “vintage”

Instead:

> **Reference sound with character.**

Technical decisions should be easy to understand. Tonal character comes from the capsule, acoustics, circuitry and voicing—not from “audio voodoo.”

## Brand Values

### Precision

Measurable, justifiable engineering: low self-noise, clean off-axis response, high dynamic range and consistent, repeatable quality.

### Character

A subtle musical signature: natural, detailed and slightly warm, without heavy coloration.

### Craft

Premium materials, precise mechanics, clear typography, engravings and visible technical care.

### Longevity

Timeless design, repairability and products that remain relevant for decades.

### Transparency

Measurements, development and technical decisions are shown openly.

## Model Designations

The emotional name takes precedence:

> **M100 Meridian**

Technical variants can be added discreetly:

> **M100 Meridian 26-M**

For example:

- `26` = approximate capsule diameter
- `C` = cardioid
- `O` = omni
- `F` = figure-eight
- `M` = multi-pattern

On the microphone itself, keep it to:

> **M100**  
> **MERIDIAN**

### Background selection

| Background | Primary logo, wordmark and lockup | Monogram | Palette documentation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ivory Beige `#E9DDC6` | Standard `.svg` files | `m100-monogram.svg` | `m100-palette.svg` |
| Forest Green `#355856` | `-inverse.svg` files | `m100-monogram.svg` | `m100-palette-inverse.svg` |
| Graphite Black `#2D2D2A` | **Standard `.svg` files; do not use inverse logos** | `m100-monogram.svg` | `m100-palette.svg` |

On Forest Green, the inverse primary logo, wordmark and lockup use ivory foreground elements so the brand remains visible. Brass and copper accents retain their original colours. On Graphite Black, keep the standard green artwork as specified by the brand direction. Do not automatically switch to inverse artwork merely because a background is dark.

The standard and inverse variants share identical geometry. Their counters, mountain stripe gaps and primary beard mouth are transparent, revealing the substrate. The monogram has a filled green field and an ivory mouth. Preserve those constructions rather than adding background-coloured patches.

### Size, spacing and production

- Preserve the aspect ratio, the margin included in each viewBox and additional breathing room around the artwork. Keep unrelated text and edges outside the logo's canvas margin.
- Use the wordmark for very small digital placements, including the 24–64 px range. At 24 px, the primary peak gaps and badge roof detail cannot all resolve. Allow at least about 128 px height for a clearly detailed monogram and about 256 px width for comfortable reading of the lockup subline. Inspect the final placement at its actual size in the [preview sheet](design/preview.html).
- The monogram is intended for a visible badge height of 20–30 mm. At 24 mm, its main gold lines are approximately 0.37 mm wide and its beard rim is approximately 0.25 mm wide. Match the final dimensions to the engraving, enamel or silkscreen process; the SVGs define flat artwork, rather than machine toolpaths or engraving depth.
- Use SVG masters for production. The PNGs in `design/qc/` are visual checks and contact sheets. Export fresh raster images from the relevant SVG when a web or print workflow requires a raster format.
- Retain the approved colours, line widths, lettering and component spacing. Do not stretch the mark, redraw the zeroes, replace outlined lettering with live text, separate the divider from its lockup, or add gradients, shadows, textures or simulated metallic effects.

For viewBoxes, detailed geometric construction, font provenance and manufacturing dimensions, see the [SVG construction notes](design/README.md).
