# M100 — wording for a wider audio range

Prepared 5 October 2026. **Owner selection: Proposal 1, the engineering-led direction.** Applied to the current brand guide and project overview. Proposals 2 and 3 remain alternatives.

## Starting point

The selected name, **METZGER100 AUDIO WORKS**, already accommodates microphones, preamplifiers, amplifiers, loudspeakers and other audio equipment. **M100** remains the compact product mark. The wording around that name should now cover recording, amplification and playback equally well.

Before this wording update, the guide used **“Studio & recording equipment”** as its optional descriptor, and the naming preview used **“across the recording chain.”** Those phrases emphasised recording and gave home listening, instrument amplification and loudspeakers less room. The broader identity retains its engineering standards, workshop character and existing artwork.

## Considerations

- **Keep the umbrella broad.** “Audio equipment” is the clearest general description. “Audio electronics & acoustics” adds a technical focus while accommodating both circuits and loudspeakers. Studio, hi-fi and live sound can describe individual applications.
- **Give each product its own function.** A microphone preamp, phono preamp, power amplifier, instrument amplifier and active loudspeaker have different purposes. Use the actual function beneath the product name instead of forcing all devices into recording terminology.
- **Share the approach, allow different sounds.** Purposeful engineering, clear controls, documentation and serviceability can unite the range. Warmth, transparency, coloration and maximum output belong in individual design briefs.
- **Let “Works” carry the craft.** It supports the independent workshop character without needing to claim hand assembly, a particular place of manufacture or an established production range.
- **Match public claims to project status.** Meridian is the current development project. Other categories are possible directions. Introduce them as scope or concepts until real projects exist; state measured performance when there is evidence.
- **Extend the original families and technical variants.** The owner confirmed the original microphone size/pattern convention and asked to preserve Meridian, Vesper, Atlas and Regent across the wider range. Following rejection of ambiguous bare suffixes, the [revised naming proposal](product-naming.md) adds an explicit equipment identifier, for example **M100 Meridian MIC-23-M**. The plain functional descriptor sits separately below it, and hardware revisions are recorded separately.

## Proposal 1 — Engineering-led (recommended)

**Full name:** METZGER100 AUDIO WORKS  
**Descriptor:** Audio electronics & acoustics  
**Brand line:** Audio equipment with precision and purpose.

**Introduction**

Metzger100 Audio Works brings electronics and acoustics together to develop audio equipment. Purposeful engineering, documented decisions and serviceability guide each design, from the signal path to the sound in the room.

**Why it works:** Preserves the strongest existing positioning and makes amplification and sound reproduction explicit. It can sit comfortably on technical documentation, an amplifier front panel or speaker packaging.

**Tradeoff:** Technical language needs a listening benefit alongside it in product descriptions.

## Proposal 2 — Workshop-led

**Full name:** METZGER100 AUDIO WORKS  
**Descriptor:** Independent audio design  
**Brand line:** Sound, thoughtfully made.

**Introduction**

Metzger100 Audio Works approaches audio equipment as a craft: considered circuits, purposeful acoustic design and practical construction. Each project is developed with attention to how it sounds, how it is used and how it can be maintained.

**Why it works:** Makes the workshop identity central and suits the restrained Art Deco artwork. It accommodates electronics, speaker enclosures and mechanical accessories without turning one product category into the brand's definition.

**Tradeoff:** The introduction must carry the equipment focus; “Independent audio design” alone could also describe a design service.

## Proposal 3 — Listening-led

**Full name:** METZGER100 AUDIO WORKS  
**Descriptor:** Audio for creators and listeners  
**Brand line:** For people who listen closely.

**Introduction**

Metzger100 Audio Works develops audio equipment for people who care about what they hear. Our design choices start with the listening experience, whether a project is made to capture a performance, shape a signal or bring music into a room.

**Why it works:** Gives recording and home listening equal weight. It is accessible on a website or product introduction and connects microphones, preamps, amplifiers and loudspeakers through their use.

**Tradeoff:** Softer and less technical; product copy must establish the engineering substance.

## Comparison

| Direction | Main emphasis | Best use | Main tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Engineering-led | Electronics, acoustics and purposeful design | Core brand wording, hardware and documentation | Needs a listening benefit in product copy |
| 2. Workshop-led | Craft, construction and care | Workshop story, packaging and project introductions | Descriptor can sound like a service |
| 3. Listening-led | The creator's and listener's experience | Website introductions and listening-focused campaigns | Needs stronger technical support |

**Selected direction:** Proposal 1 extends the selected identity with the least disruption and covers the requested range. Proposal 2 offers a stronger workshop voice; Proposal 3 offers a more accessible listening voice. The existing **“Precision. Character. Built to last.”** remains a supporting statement of design intent.

| M100 [family] AMP-[technical variant] | Power, integrated or instrument amplifier — choose the actual function | Illustrative future wording |
| M100 [family] SPK-[technical variant] | Passive loudspeaker, active loudspeaker or studio monitor — choose the actual function | Illustrative future wording |
| M100 [family] [equipment identifier]-[technical variant] | Actual function, such as equalizer, audio interface or monitor controller | Illustrative future wording |

Each product introduction should state its purpose first, then its particular sonic intent and relevant evidence. Category examples do not assign names, specifications or a release roadmap.

## Applied wording

The selected descriptor and introduction are applied to the [brand guide](../README.md) and [project overview](../../README.md). The guide's manifesto and equipment examples now include amplification and sound reproduction. The [naming preview](preview.html) and its [generator](../design/tools/build_branding_proposal.py) use the broader scope and technical-variant hierarchy.

The logo subline already reads METZGER100 AUDIO WORKS. No new logo subline is required for any of these wording directions.
