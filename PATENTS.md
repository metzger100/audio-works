# Patent review and implementation gates

Owner requirement, 2026-10-05/2026-10-09: avoid third-party infringement. Scope: hardware/software, signal conversion, power/bias, manufacture and build/implementation guidance. No clearance asserted.

## Claims and territories

Screen actual implementations/activities. European claims use description/drawings ([EPC 69](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/a69.html)); record territorial status, states and enforcement ([DPMA](https://www.dpma.de/english/patents/patent_protection/index.html), [EPC 64](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/a64.html)).

Map each right: Germany/relevant Europe first, then manufacture/use/supply/distribution territories. DE/EP/US/WO searches, originality, unfamiliar circuits, value changes, application notes, disclosure, open licensing and private DIY confer no worldwide/automatic clearance. Copyright/model redistribution and engineering qualification stay separate.

## Required evidence

1. Candidate/version, features, activities, territories and date. Cover relevant charge/current sensing, bootstrapping, floating/differential sensing, servos, common-mode power extraction, output drive and carrier demodulation.
2. Reproducible feature/classification searches in official databases/registers; inspect families, granted/amended claims and pending applications. Results/resemblance establish no coverage conclusion.
3. Independent elements, dependent limitations, interpretation/equivalents uncertainty and overlap/distinction rationale. Refer material uncertainty for professional review.
4. Publication/grant IDs, family, priority/filing dates, territory, official status source/date, term/status evidence and relied-on licences/authorizations. Old dates, third-party badges or one inactive member prove no expiry/freedom to operate.
5. Distinguish territorial expired/non-enforceable prior art, no blocking claim found in a defined search, potentially relevant claims, unresolved risks and documented resolutions. State limits/unexamined areas; no blanket guarantee.
6. Refresh after structural changes, relevant substitutions or territory/status changes; preserve originals/decisions.

## Implementation and release gates

Flag features early; complete claim/status screening before affected prototype selection/manufacture/build or implementation publication. Hold credible unresolved potentially blocking risks; retain experiments/memory/patent-dependent families and continue unaffected research.

Resolve holds through evidence-backed alternatives, applicable documented licensing/authorization or suitable professional assessment. Match authorization to implementation/activities/territory. Check alternatives against claims and rerun engineering tests; cosmetic redraws/renaming/value changes prove no design-around.

Report scope/status beside simulation/procurement/physical evidence. Performance/novelty/cost/SPICE cannot override holds. Internal records/unscreened benchmarks confer no clearance or public build recommendation.

## Records and limits

The [track](meridian-microphone/lab/research/patents/README.md) and [register](meridian-microphone/lab/research/patents/register.yaml) retain candidate-linked searches, mappings, sources, review dates, risks and resolutions. Agents record evidence/risks, never non-infringement opinions/global “patent safe” labels. Material interpretation/coverage/status uncertainty requires professional review before held release. Finding none proves none absent. Screening is incomplete. Mechanical checks make no legal determination.
