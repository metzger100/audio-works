# Patent review and implementation gates

Owner requirement, 2026-10-05; restated 2026-10-09: avoid third-party infringement alongside engineering, reproducibility and procurement. Covers hardware/software, signal conversion, power/bias, manufacture and build/implementation guidance. Existing artifacts are not asserted cleared.

## Claims and territories

Screen actual implementations and intended activities. European claims use description/drawings: [EPC 69](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/a69.html). Record territorial status, states and enforcement: [DPMA](https://www.dpma.de/english/patents/patent_protection/index.html), [EPC 64](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/a64.html).

Start with Germany/relevant European rights; add manufacture, use, supply and distribution territories. DE/EP/US/WO family searches establish no worldwide clearance; map each right. Originality, unfamiliar circuits, changed values, application notes, disclosure, open licensing and private DIY establish no automatic clearance. Copyright/model redistribution and engineering qualification remain separate.

## Required evidence

1. Identify candidate/version, features, activities, territories and date. Include relevant charge/current sensing, bootstrapping, floating/differential sensing, servos, common-mode power extraction, output drive and carrier demodulation.
2. Record reproducible feature/classification searches in official databases/registers. Inspect relevant families, granted/amended claims and pending applications. Search results or resemblance establish no coverage conclusion.
3. Map independent claim elements and dependent limitations; record interpretation/equivalents uncertainty and overlap/distinction rationale. Refer material uncertainty for professional review.
4. Record publication/grant IDs, family, priority/filing dates, territory, official status source/date, term/status evidence and relied-on licences/authorizations. Old dates, third-party badges or one inactive member establish no expiry/freedom to operate.
5. Distinguish territorial expired/non-enforceable prior art, no blocking claim identified in a defined search, potentially relevant claims, unresolved risks and documented resolutions. State limits/unexamined areas; none is a blanket guarantee.
6. Refresh after structural changes, relevant substitutions or territory/status changes. Preserve original evidence and decisions.

## Implementation and release gates

Flag concrete features early. Complete claim/status screening before affected prototype selection, manufacture or build/implementation publication. Hold credible unresolved potentially blocking risks. Preserve experiments/memory and patent-dependent families; continue unaffected research.

Resolve holds through evidence-backed alternatives, applicable documented licensing/authorization or suitable professional assessment. Authorization must match implementation, activities and territory; contacts, payments, licence acceptance and publication require separate authorization. Assess alternatives against claims and rerun engineering tests. Cosmetic redraws, renaming or value changes prove no design-around.

Report screening scope/status alongside simulation, procurement and physical evidence. Performance, novelty, cost and SPICE cannot override holds. Internal records/unscreened benchmarks are not cleared implementations or public build recommendations.
