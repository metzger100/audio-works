# M100 Meridian

Original condenser-microphone research for private DIY use in Germany: accordion, ukulele, acoustic instruments, voice-over and distant male-choir recording. Priorities are low noise, accurate transients, natural detail, smooth off-axis behavior and restrained warmth; transparency takes precedence over added distortion.

[Current state](../CURRENT.md) · [Decisions](DECISIONS.md) · [Detailed brief and capsule history](BRIEF.md) · [Laboratory](lab/README.md)

The owner specified Arienne Audio Flat K47 Cardioid/Omni K47FRB for simulation on 2026-10-05. Earlier capsule-choice gates are superseded for authorized simulation research. Purchases, safe physical polarization and production/prototype selection remain separate unresolved matters.

The semiconductor prerequisite is partial and behavior-specific: OPA197 has scoped grounded small-signal agreement; complete production/noise, floating/nonlinear and P48 output/power coverage is unavailable. See the [model report](lab/research/device_model_validation.md). Passing software controls does not qualify a microphone.
