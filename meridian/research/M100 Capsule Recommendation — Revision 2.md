# M100 Capsule Recommendation — Revision 2

**Project:** M100 Meridian · **Milestone:** capsule research only · **Checked:** 4 October 2026, Europe/Berlin · **Status:** one capsule recommended; owner selection and purchase pending.

## Recommendation

**Choose one Arienne Audio Flat K47, in the normal Cardioid/Omni variant, reference K47FRB, for the finished M100.**

[Purchase listing with the relevant variant selected](https://store.arienneaudio.com/microphone-capsules/20-26-arienne-audio-flat-k47-microphone-capsule.html#/12-options-cardioid_omni).

The live store showed **€116.74**, **11 units**, and single-unit ordering. This is the displayed merchandise price; German shipping and the exact tax treatment remain unverified. The cheaper cardioid-only variant was on backorder. Do not substitute the regular K47, a used early revision, or the Reject Lottery option. Do not pay for pair matching or buy extra capsules for selection.

This is my engineering choice for the complete brief, **not a finding that the capsule has been proven to meet every target**. Its strongest case is its deliberately restrained presence response, usable directional/omni options, accessible bare-capsule format, current retail availability, and cost. Its weakest case is the missing calibrated noise, overload, and angular data. The independent evidence supports taking a considered risk on this component; it does not support promising a ≤7 dBA, ≥135 dB SPL microphone.

The intended outcome is to retain this capsule in the M100 and develop around its measured properties. If its intrinsic noise or angular response disappoints, the honest consequence may be a revised performance target. A later circuit cannot guarantee a cure. No comparison purchases are recommended.

German, European, US, Australian and Japanese-manufacturer routes were considered alongside eBay and marketplace leads. Evidence labels are **V**: inspected page/drawing/listing/arithmetic; **M**: manufacturer or seller claim; **C**: community report, with measured results distinguished from listening; **U**: unknown; **I**: engineering inference. V does not mean that a published acoustic claim was independently tested. Manufacturer statements posted in forums remain M.

## Why this is the choice

**I — A useful balance across the intended recordings.** A fixed pressure omni is attractive for natural distant recording, but gives no directional alternative for voice-over or recording an instrument in an ordinary room. A small cardioid is attractive for choir coverage, but the accessible well-documented examples do not establish the unusually low noise target. The Flat K47's cardioid/omni version leaves two useful acoustic operating choices in a single retained component. That does not lock the eventual pattern-switching implementation.

**I — Warmth through the acoustic response is a better starting point for this brief.** The reduced upper-mid emphasis is consistent with the requested natural, slightly warm sound. Existing listening reports give it more support than an anonymous “flat response” sales graph. We are choosing that acoustic direction, not a vintage microphone circuit or an assumption that distortion adds quality.

**I — Paying more did not resolve the crucial uncertainties.** The Thiersch, BeesNeez, Peluso and premium DIY capsule routes have meaningful construction or manufacturing arguments, but none of the inspected bare-capsule offers supplied a demonstrable all-target advantage that justified choosing it solely by price or reputation. The Flat K47 leaves more of the budget available for completing the actual microphone.

**I — A large membrane is a low-noise opportunity, not proof.** Larger condenser membranes often help the noise/sensitivity tradeoff, while smaller acoustic structures generally help high-frequency angular behavior. Actual results also depend on damping, gap, tension, cavities and surrounding structures. Exact current Flat K47 dimensions are not established here. Do not turn the model name into a 34 mm active-diaphragm specification. [Neumann's explanation of diaphragm size and the tradeoffs](https://www.neumann.com/ko-kr/knowledge-base/neumann-in-the-homestudio/homestudio-academy/what-is-the-difference-between-large-and-small-diaphragm-microphones/).

This recommendation prioritizes the combination of quiet-source potential, restrained tonal balance, pattern flexibility, and a practical single-unit price. It accepts more acoustic uncertainty than the Haun route. It does **not** claim that Flat K47 is the most thoroughly documented choir capsule.

## Alternatives tested against the recommendation

These are documentary comparisons. **Do not order any of them alongside Flat K47.**

### Haun KA 200 N: strongest German purchasing route

The KA200N remains the most credible low-logistics-risk alternative, with a small modular head, manufacturer angular plots and a longstanding Thomann offer. The checked price is €239 including VAT, with German standard shipping free at that basket value. [Thomann listing](https://www.thomann.de/de/haun_ka_200_n_282354.htm).

**M — historical complete systems:** Haun's 2002 catalog gives KA200N/MBP603(A) as 14 mV/Pa, 12 dBA and 130 dB maximum SPL; other amplifiers produce different published sensitivity/noise. These are not isolated capsule limits or promises for M100. The raw capacitance, polarization and interface remain insufficiently documented for an original design. [Archived catalog](reference-datasheets/Haun-catalog.pdf), [maker product information](https://www.mbho.de/products03_22.htm), [manufacturer polar graphic](https://www.mbho.de/images/charts/ka200n_polar.gif).

**I — why I do not choose it for the whole brief:** better-supported choir acoustics and German procurement do not establish the unusually quiet system target, nor the requested overload margin. Its manufacturer's established system figures point to a compromise we would knowingly accept at a higher capsule cost. That would be reasonable for a choir-first brief; it does not displace the current combined-purpose choice. A 21 mm head OD is not its diaphragm diameter.

### Primo EM21 / EM23: important medium electret alternative

**V/M — EM21:** MicBooster's current live page offers one **Primo EM21 cardioid**, SKU FC235, at **£37.60** with “in stock” shown. Its PDF is dated September 2000. It is a FET-less electret: the test FET shown is external. The drawing gives 21 mm maximum front OD, a 19 mm body section, 9 mm main depth plus a 4.5 mm rear projection, M20.3×0.5 mounting thread and a rear gold contact. Active membrane diameter is not identified. Published values are −40 dBV/Pa ±2.5 dB without circuit gain, 55–70 pF at 1 kHz/1 V, and SNR above 70 dB A referenced to 1 Pa. A 180° response trace is supplied; a full polar map and maximum acoustic SPL are absent. [Current offer](https://micbooster.com/product/primo-em21-cardioid-microphone-capsule/), [manufacturer sheet](https://micbooster.com/datasheets/EM21.pdf), [archived original](reference-datasheets/Primo-EM21.pdf).

**I — interpreting those figures:** −40 dBV/Pa corresponds to approximately 10 mV/Pa. The published SNR establishes only an equivalent level below approximately 24 dBA in its specified context. It does not substantiate 7 dBA. The test circuit's supply voltage is not a capsule polarization requirement or rating. Do not carry its obsolete example transistor into our parts plan.

**C — promising but not a unit guarantee:** Scott Wurcer reported EM23/EM21 recording systems reaching 8–10 dBA in 2021, while saying EM21 seemed unavailable then. His 2017 teardown and low-noise description specifically concern the omni EM23. These are valuable firsthand engineering reports, but not a separate calibrated acceptance specification for every EM21 now sold. [2021 firsthand report, post #84](https://www.diyaudio.com/community/threads/fixed-gain-field-recorder.373352/page-5), [2017 EM23 teardown, post #35](https://www.diyaudio.com/community/threads/electret-condenser-mics.306237/page-2).

**V/M — EM23:** its manufacturer sheet specifies an omni electret with 56–58 pF and similar nominal sensitivity. The old retail URL is now a missing page; the current shop search returned its housing and EM21, not EM23 itself. This does not prove global discontinuation. [EM23 technical sheet](https://micbooster.com/datasheets/EM23.pdf), [current shop search](https://micbooster.com/?s=EM23&post_type=product).

**I — why they do not displace the choice:** EM21 deserves more consideration than Revision 1 gave it. Its price, medium size, identified maker and documented interface are attractive. However, replenishment/current manufacture and storage age are not confirmed; a second reliable small-quantity route was not established. Its own angular and overload data still leave important risks. EM23 also loses directional versatility and lacks a verified current retail offer. Neither is rejected simply for being an electret.

### Transound TSB-2555B / JLI-2555BXZ3-GP: affordable, but missing target evidence

The documented external-FET electret architecture and several retail channels remain useful strengths. The EU mounted offer was €38 excluding VAT; bare JLI was $12.95. The JLI fixture specifies 120 dB SPL at <1% THD, while the UK seller claims 135 dB without resolving the conditions. Its quoted SNR also belongs to a test context. **No verified 7 dBA/135 dB combination follows from these statements.** [JLI retail](https://www.jlielectronics.com/microphone-capsules/jli-2555bxz3-gp/), [manufacturer/distributor sheet](reference-datasheets/JLI-2555BXZ3-GP.pdf), [UK offer](https://micbooster.com/product/jli2555/).

### BeesNeez BN2: interesting original architecture, insufficient metrology

**M:** the BN2 is an edge-terminated original design; the multipattern version has a central vented structure. The maker describes an aluminized Styroflex diaphragm and smooth balance. The capsule-range page shows cardioid at AUD265 and multipattern at AUD420; a multipattern detail page showed AUD400, so the price discrepancy requires checkout verification. **U:** active diameter, capacitance, calibrated noise/SPL and frequency-dependent polars were not established. ## Germany cost and the single-unit purchase

**Recommended quantity: one.** The two/four-unit calculations in Revision 1 remain reference arithmetic only. There is no planned self-selection batch, comparison bundle or spare capsule.

For K47FRB, the only verified current figure is the **€116.74 product display**. A reliable landed quotation could not be established from the publicly visible information. Preserve that uncertainty rather than replacing it with a fabricated shipping price.

If that displayed amount were an export **net** goods price, freight were **€20**, an affected low-value parcel incurred **€3 duty**, no import tax had been prepaid and no carrier charge applied, the approximate landed cost would be:

`1.19 × (116.74 + 20 + 3) = €166.29`.

This is an **I — conditional budget example**, not a German quote or an authorized spending limit. If seller tax is already included, the goods basis must first be corrected; if German VAT is collected at checkout, do not add it again. Actual freight, exchange/card spreads and carrier fees may change the result.

The July 2026 low-value import change is material: affected consignments up to €150 can incur the temporary €3 duty by tariff category, rather than retaining the old blanket duty exemption. The declaration regime and any applicable exception matter. Higher-value consignments require the ordinary tariff assessment, not an assumed €3 charge. German import VAT is normally 19%. [European Commission guidance](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/guidance-and-legal-text-temporary-flat-fee-low-value-imports-which-will-apply-until-1-july-2028-2026-06-08_en), [German Finance Ministry](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Standardartikel/Themen/Zoll/wegfall-der-150-euro-zollfreigrenze.html).

Before paying, the owner's actual basket should show the exact variant, quantity one, delivery to Germany, freight, tax/duty collection and total. Returns/defect replacement after installation remain a material commercial unknown. This recommendation does not depend on a free trial, return after audition, or resale recovering the expenditure.

## Remaining questions and how we handle them

| Question | Can development accommodate it? | Remaining consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What are the supplied connections and electrical isolation? | Yes, once identified; check before connecting anything. | Misidentification can damage the retained component or produce false measurements. |
| What are its capacitance and sensitivity? | Usually measurable and usable to guide later development. | They affect the achievable noise and gain; adjustment does not make their value irrelevant. |
| What bias is safe and intended for this batch? | Obtain an existing maker limit if available; otherwise use a conservative, non-destructive characterization approach once authorized. | Do not infer a current maximum from an older batch. No voltage is selected here. |
| What is the membrane's intrinsic noise? | Measure the assembled system carefully and distinguish environmental/electronic noise. | Electronics cannot remove capsule mechanical noise. ≤7 dBA remains aspirational. |
| Does it handle approximately 135 dB SPL with low THD? | Characterization can distinguish sensor and electronics overload. | If the membrane itself limits performance, a later electronic pad cannot guarantee a cure. |
| Is response smooth at the choir's actual angles? | Measure angular behavior and later grille interaction using the retained part. | A single on-axis EQ cannot remove arbitrary angle-dependent coloration. |
| How much do the rear acoustic paths and surroundings matter? | Preserve acoustic access; compare measurements before/after eventual integration. | Do not lock a body from a capsule-family name or a vintage reference. |
| Will another identical unit be obtainable? | Record model, batch, serial and any supplied measurement record. | Repeatability is a supplier/revision risk, not a circuit problem. |
| What is the actual German landed total and return policy? | Resolve in the actual basket/published terms before payment. | The example above is not a guarantee or a purchase authorization. |

**I — target discipline:** the total noise includes the capsule and electronics. For independent contributions with the same weighting, two equal 7 dBA contributions produce approximately 10 dBA total. A mechanical floor above 7 dBA rules out a 7 dBA total regardless of converter quality. Conversely, no published noise result is not proof of an excellent floor. High sensitivity also does not establish low distortion at high sound pressure.
