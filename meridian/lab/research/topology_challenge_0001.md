# Initial architecture challenge

22 recorded concepts: one conventional control plus 21 research proposals. The minimal-active JFET proposal also retains a conventional voltage input. 20 concepts avoid a conventional JFET voltage-buffer input; scientific mechanisms seed 17 proposals. None has been simulated or declared viable. All 14 required families have persistent records.

The dominant assumptions being challenged are voltage sensing, a FET first device, a fixed capsule reference, shared AC/DC control paths and mirrored output paths. Nine physical questions per concept are recorded in [concepts.yaml](../search/concepts.yaml). Engineer implementation and SPICE falsification come next.
