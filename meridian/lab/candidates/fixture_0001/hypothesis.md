# Infrastructure fixture

This tests the environment and measurement code against known physical/analytic relationships. Ideal buffers deliberately expose measurement behavior without a semiconductor population. The passive P48 power load is separate from those ideal buffers. Energy accounting is therefore incomplete. It cannot be ranked, qualified, or described as a viable microphone, however good its simulated audio metrics look.

The capsule is supplied from the **loaded internal rail** through 100 MΩ. No ideal 48 V internal supply or ideal 60 V polarization is inserted. Native charge-law capacitors conserve charge under the prescribed mechanical motion. Rear electrical capacitance is present, while rear acoustic pressure is zero. This does not assert a cardioid acoustic model.
