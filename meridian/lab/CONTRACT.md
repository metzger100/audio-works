# Meridian research agent contract

Follow the user's mission and phase order. Physics/measurements > SPICE > tests > numerical optimization > reasoning > intuition. Work autonomously on research/code; never purchase or contact suppliers without separate authorization.

Patent non-infringement is a mandatory owner requirement. Read `../../PATENTS.md` and `research/patents/README.md`. Record early candidate-feature flags and separate dated claim/territory/current-status evidence. Complete applicable screening and resolve credible potentially blocking risks before prototype selection, manufacture or publishing implementation/build guidance. Keep affected implementations on hold while material patent risks are unresolved; preserve their experiments and continue unaffected research. Do not infer clearance from novelty, open licensing, private DIY use or SPICE performance, and do not claim legal non-infringement. Refer material legal uncertainty for appropriate professional review before release. This is an instruction/review gate, not an implemented automated legal check.

Standard, documented and currently obtainable parts are a core repository requirement. Read `../../COMPONENTS.md`. Use ordinary production parts, obtainable nominal values, practical packages and documented tolerances. Do not make NOS, obsolete, selected or undocumented parts prerequisites. Continuous optimizer results remain proposals until an actual BOM is recorded and the complete applicable suite is repeated with its values, variation and relevant parasitics. Validate substitutes, preserve the continuous parent and record each implementation revision. Retain unsourced concepts in the research archive, but resolve implementation dependencies before prototype selection or build recommendations. This policy is a review requirement, not an implemented automatic sourcing check.

Read the parent `../README.md` and `../DECISIONS.md` procurement requirements and `research/procurement_germany.md`. Implementation parts must be privately orderable in small quantities for delivery to Germany; record current sourcing evidence and intended-quantity delivered EUR costs with shipping/tax/import assumptions. Keep procurement readiness separate from simulation performance. International retail routes remain eligible. Do not recommend unused comparison purchases, bulk selection or additional capsules without a finished-project use and owner decision. No numerical spending cap is established; do not invent one.

Read `README.md`, `spec/*.yaml`, `research/sources.yaml`, `research/rejected_ideas.md` and `research/discoveries.md`. Preserve the Inventor and Engineer roles in `research/roles.md`; no role can declare success without evidence. Keep all 14 families alive. Proposals are not results. Never clone a known production microphone to seed the search; strong conventional references are fair benchmarks in Phase 3.

Run `./run verify` after relevant code/model edits and `./run evaluate <id>` for a candidate. Exit 2 means unqualified, even if some screens pass. Never relax thresholds, hide missing models, narrow uncertain ranges, discard convergence errors or optimize against a single favorable specimen. Use documented bounds and numerical optimization. Keep simultaneous evaluators on stable code/model sources.

Do not overwrite candidate parent netlists. Register structural children using `search/mutate.py` and record the hypothesis. Keep raw data, failures and parent comparisons. Preserve source/spec snapshots and hashes. The quality-diversity archive retains niches; novelty cannot grant performance or eligibility. No eligible Pareto winner until complete qualification and measurement evidence.

Unknown safe capsule polarization prohibits interpreting simulation voltage as a hardware rating. Current model has no valid mechanical-noise/SPL or polar-response claims. Device model/noise/temperature gaps, return-ratio stability and full-corner qualification remain explicit incomplete checks. Upgrade these before advancing toward prototypes.

Spec amendments require separate evidence and a recorded revision. The user may authorize changes, but never silently modify the lock to make a failed candidate pass. Update phase reports and research memory as evidence changes. Physical measurement adapters must preserve raw records, calibration and uncertainty and use the same metric/status contract.
