## JFET choices and matching

[TI JFE2140](https://www.ti.com/product/JFE2140) is an ordinary integrated dual, independent of JFE150 as a device but sharing TI as manufacturer. Its [SLPS730B sheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/jfe2140.pdf) specifies conditioned low-current noise and matching; 25°C VGS mismatch is at most 4mV at 2mA, full-temperature bound 4.2mV, and Idss ratio 0.95–1.05. These are ordinary production bounds, not selected-pair permission. The public PSpice and TINA archives have byte-identical `jfex140.lib` members. The vendor instructs two half-model instances for one physical package; identical simulated halves do **not** model guaranteed mismatch, thermal coupling or a distribution. Explicit documentary coupled bounds were added without fitting synthetic process corners.

The stable control found Idss 12.296mA at VDS10V/VGS0/25°C inside 12–23mA. Voltage noise at 1kHz was 2.152nV/√Hz at 100µA and 1.053nV/√Hz at 2mA, within the existing 20% typical-agreement screen. At 10Hz it was 2.223/1.191nV/√Hz versus conditioned typical 5.4/2.4: both disagree. Current noise at VDS5V/2mA/1kHz was 0.488fA/√Hz versus 1.6 typical. The model's gate-drain diode has BV10V; simulated reverse-gate leakage at VGS−30V/VDS0 was 105.6µA versus the sheet's 60pA 25°C bound. Microampere gate currents also appear at VDS10V. These are disqualifying leakage-model discrepancies, not measured device behavior. Clamp rails and the original failed low-VCH fixture are preserved separately. Capacitance is recorded without resolving the sheet's missing explicit frequency/gate-bias condition.

[Linear Systems LSK170](https://www.linearsystems.com/jfetamplifierssingles/lsk170-series) supplies a genuinely independent manufacturer and a standard A grade, without screening services. The current public model is a dated 16 March 2026 PDF; its exact literal transcription retains native noise terms NLEV3/GDSNOI8. The original MFG metadata token failed parsing. An authored child removes only that non-electrical token, preserving the original/PDF hashes and all electrical parameters. The [ngspice manual](https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/docs/ngspice-46-manual.pdf) documents these JFET noise terms; no generic noise fit was substituted. Model Idss was 4.475mA within A-grade 2.6–6.5mA. At 2mA/VDS10V it gave 1.382nV/√Hz at 1kHz and 2.017 at 10Hz versus sheet typical 0.9/1.4; both fail the typical screen, although below listed 1.9/4.0 maxima. Ciss was 25.05pF at 15V/100µA/1MHz versus 20pF typical, also outside the screen. Reverse-leakage below a maximum does not validate its temperature/high-field law. This is usable for restricted DC and discrepancy experiments, not microphone noise prediction.

[LSK389](https://www.linearsystems.com/jfet-amplifiers-duals/lsk389-series) adds another dual family. The May 2026 model PDF contains four grade models; only A was transcribed for the exact researched SOIC order code. No four-device count or matching service follows. The 2023 sheet's 15mV VGS match at 1mA/10V and Idss ratio 0.9–1.1 are preserved. Its noise marketing/table and note 3 contain conflicting guarantee language; no production-noise guarantee is inferred. Model acquired, behavior uncharacterized; physical substrate/pin integration remains pending.

Thus two manufacturers provide acquired JFET equations, with JFE150, JFE2140, LSK170A and LSK389A representing four distinct device types. None supplies a fully validated input/charge/noise/production set. onsemi J113/MMBFJ113, MMBFJ201 and InterFET IF170/IF3602 remain leads without exact acquired validated models. IF170 campaign spectra at 5mA/3V or grade-A 0.8Idss cannot be transferred to 100µA Meridian bias. 2SK170, BF862 and 2N5457 are historical search leads; current official lifecycle was not established and none became an implementation dependency.

## Ordinary BJTs, MOS/CMOS and support controls

**5551/5401 parts are BJTs.** [Nexperia PMBT5551](https://www.nexperia.com/product/PMBT5551), exact `PMBT5551,215`, is a 160V NPN SOT-23 option with an original self-contained model. Model C/B/E order differs from physical B1/E2/C3. At 5V/25°C, beta was 143 at 1mA and 121 at 10mA, above corresponding minimum 80. Temperature/current grids −10/25/50°C and 100µA/1/2/10mA are retained. The 52V fixed-base-temperature stress produced 114mA at 102°C: that invalidates the assumed 2mA/104mW thermal point and exceeds package power by a wide margin. It demonstrates the need for current feedback; it is not a safe operating point or electrothermal solution. Open-emitter capacitance and 25°C ICBO fixtures emitted strict convergence warnings; no cap/leakage validation credit is assigned from those outputs. KF is absent.
