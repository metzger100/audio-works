1. Use actual nominal values and documented semiconductor models before any optimization. Retain incomplete device/noise/process coverage rather than treating a nominal curve as production yield. Ordinary passives and intended one-microphone quantities avoid unused comparison purchases.
2. Give the conventional JFET reference a discrete real-output child after CMOS macro convergence failure. Preserve the source-follower mechanism and equal test effort. A failed common output macro cannot disprove JFET conversion.
3. Replace the feedback JFET's failed CMOS slow servo with a recorded discrete NPN differential controller. This preserves separate DC current feedback and exposes finite base-current/divider/reference errors. Keep the original parent and results.
4. Preserve guarded CMOS and charge-feedback conversion in output-stage children. Do not relabel an ideal input experiment as CMOS hardware evidence; unresolved macro behavior remains an implementation/model gap.
5. Preserve the true all-NPN audio/power path. Test a separate500kΩ reset branch realized with two parallel1MΩ production resistors for increased DC authority, documenting the charge-gain/noise tradeoff. No trimming, matching or selection is allowed.
6. Retain carrier excitation/product controls as explicit ideals. Add a distinct deterministic periodic protocol and independently calculable sideband controls. Mark noise/power/clipping/full qualification unsupported until physical periodic device behavior and stochastic protocol exist.
7. Resolve backordered passive dependencies through separately recorded AC10kΩ and C0G100pF children with full supported reevaluation, retaining parent dependencies. Same nominal value is not substitute-equivalence evidence; missing loss/noise/rating/source details keep build readiness held.
8. Freeze the BOM and rationale with netlist/metadata, not just the simulator sources. Add terminal energy closure without lowering existing budgets. Preserve every failed model, registration, initialization and simulation record.
9. Keep patent screening independent of simulation and technical-source reading. Early candidate-linked features are unscreened; no official claim/status investigation or non-infringement guarantee is asserted. Resolve material legal uncertainty professionally before affected selection.
10. Verify/inventory new evidence and capture/restore available bytes. Same-device recovery and declared89 legacy-source gaps cannot satisfy independent persistent preservation. Keep that limitation visible in current facts.
