## Three required mechanism tracks

1. **0012 BJT-only charge/reset control.** Derive charge conversion with finite loop gain/bandwidth, reset impedance, base-current DC authority, device capacitance and input current/flicker noise. Explain the existing poor transfer/response and the failed 0024 child using independent equations and parameter-identifiability checks. Do not call tiny electrode voltage a lost charge signal without the conversion calculation. Create a bounded isolated reset/charge oracle before an actual standard-parts structural child. Preserve a no-FET mechanism where feasible; if conversion changes, record a new mechanism rather than quietly replacing it with a JFET buffer.
2. **0010/0014 CMOS floating-supply and guard/charge controls.** Derive expected DC currents, common-mode range, reset/bias authority, input admittance/noise gain and loop behavior. Build a grounded reference fixture, then translate both rails, input/output references and loads together. Test terminal-level invariance and supply/ground current with the actual macro; identify global-ground internal dependencies, limiting switches or unsupported nonlinear regions. Reference translation is a numerical/physical oracle, not permission to rewrite vendor internals until the circuit works. Preserve originals; any correction/alternative creates a documented child with independent validation. Separately test guard tracking, parasitic positive feedback, capacitive loading and reset settling. An ideal control can diagnose the fixture but cannot receive CMOS performance or noise ratings.
3. **0013 carrier periodic-noise control.** Retain the successful isolated deterministic bridge control and failed integrated P48 parent. Derive sidebands/demodulation and a simple analytically calculable noisy carrier/bridge/mixer/filter example, including source spectra, modulation gain, image/alias/folding terms, correlations and estimator bandwidth. Verify recovery/zero/carrier-off controls, then independently verify noise power with a supported periodic or explicitly seeded time-domain method. Research actual backend capabilities and alternatives; ordinary stationary `.noise` is not a carrier architecture rating. Preserve seeds, white/colored-source calibration, sampling/window/timestep/settling refinements and PSD confidence. If no supported protocol meets the existing constraints, deliver the analytical oracle, retained tests and precise missing prerequisite without fabricated noise numbers. Real oscillator/buffer/mixer/output hardware, dynamic power, loading and RF-safe capsule conditions remain unresolved until actually modeled/evidenced.
