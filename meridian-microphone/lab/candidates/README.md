## Qualification conditions

Every current candidate remains unqualified. No selection, trimming, substitute equivalence, production-noise or patent clearance is claimed. Explicit ideal blocks remain ineligible. Nominal passives and tolerance stresses are implemented; unmeasured ESR, leakage, excess noise and temperature laws remain gaps. Actual BOMs describe intended-quantity research, with live availability and delivered costs unresolved. Board parasitics are assumptions. Engineering, procurement, patent and physical gates remain separate.
